Pulse uses smartctl to read drive health (SMART data). Pulse runs smartctl as a
separate program, through its command line. It does not link to smartctl and
does not modify it. Where smartctl is bundled it sits at
Pulse.app/Contents/Helpers/smartctl; otherwise Pulse uses a Homebrew install.

The GPL-2.0 licence applies to smartctl itself and not to Pulse.
